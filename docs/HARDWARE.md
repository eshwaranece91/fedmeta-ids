# Hardware requirements

## Minimum

- CPU: 8 cores
- RAM: 16 GB
- Disk: 50 GB
- OS: Ubuntu 22.04

## Recommended

- CPU: 32 cores
- RAM: 64 GB
- GPU: NVIDIA RTX 4090 (24 GB)
- Disk: 200 GB SSD

## Runtime

| Configuration | CPU-only | With GPU |
|---|---|---|
| Smoke (1 seed, 50 nodes) | 5 min | 2 min |
| Full (30 seeds, 50 nodes) | 8 h | 1 h |
| Full (30 seeds, 500 nodes) | 48 h | 6 h |
